# GulfCore Business Solutions LLC

## Company Overview

GulfCore Business Solutions LLC is a fictional Dubai-based
business solutions company providing IT services, technical
support and operational services to corporate customers.

This project uses a simulated company dataset to demonstrate
practical Data Analyst and Business Analyst skills.

## Business Departments

1. Sales & Business Development
2. Finance & Accounts
3. Human Resources
4. IT & Technical Support
5. Operations & Customer Service

## Business Scenario

Senior management has requested a consolidated Q3 2026
performance report covering July, August and September.

Currently, departmental information is maintained separately.
Management needs a single report to understand financial
performance, sales achievement, employee productivity,
operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

## Project Objective

To consolidate and analyse business data using Microsoft Excel,
identify performance gaps, investigate their causes and provide
actionable recommendations to management.

## Project Period

July 2026 – September 2026

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel Online
- Pivot Tables
- Excel formulas
- Charts and KPI dashboards
- GitHub for project documentation and version control
